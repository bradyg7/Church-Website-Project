What is Celebrate Recovery?

Celebrate Recovery is a Christ-centered, 12-step recovery program for anyone struggling with hurt, hang-ups, and habits of any kind. Celebrate Recovery features large-group meetings that include worship and teaching, followed by small-group discussions where participants share their experiences and offer mutual support. The program emphasizes personal accountability, spiritual growth, and the healing power of Christ. By fostering a non-judgmental environment, Celebrate Recovery aims to help individuals find freedom from their struggles and build a healthier, more fulfilling life.
